File Resurge Privacy Policy

File Resurge 1.1.0 and later · Effective date: 13 November 2026

Summary

File Resurge (package name com.tavirox.nelqura.delrev) is an Android app that finds photos, videos, audio files and documents on your phone, including hidden copies and items in the system trash, saves copies of the files you choose, and helps you delete junk files. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of File Resurge.

Scanning, previewing, recovering and cleaning happen entirely on your phone. Your files, their names and their contents are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. File Resurge has no accounts and no servers of its own. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics; Google collects some data to provide those services, as described below.

Information processed on your device

To do its job, File Resurge reads the following on your phone only:

  • Files in shared storage: the names, folders, sizes and dates of photos, videos, audio files and documents, including hidden files and files in trash folders. File Resurge does not read files inside other apps' private folders (Android/data and Android/obb).
  • The system trash list: on Android 11 and later, the items you moved to the trash, so they can be shown and copied.
  • Installer (APK) files: the version name and number written inside APK files found in storage, shown in Clean Storage.
  • File contents for duplicate detection: photos of the same size are compared by a checksum calculated on your phone. The checksums are not stored or sent anywhere.

Scan results are kept only in memory while the app is open. File Resurge stores a few settings in its private storage: whether you have chosen a language, whether you have finished the introduction, and when the last full-screen ad was shown. These settings may be included in your phone's own Android backup.

Recovering, sharing and deleting files

  • Recover: copies the files you select into Pictures/FileResurge, Movies/FileResurge, Music/FileResurge or Documents/FileResurge on your phone. The original files are not changed.
  • Share and Open with: when you tap these, Android lets you choose an app, and only the files you selected are given to that app.
  • Delete and Clean: nothing is deleted until you select it and confirm. File Resurge then reports only the files and space that were really removed.

Permissions

  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), Android 11 and later: needed to find hidden files and items in the system trash anywhere in shared storage, to save recovered copies and to delete the files you choose. The app explains this before opening the Android setting, and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings. Without it, scanning does not start.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), Android 9 and 10 only: the same purpose on older Android versions.
  • Internet and network state: for ads, consent, analytics and crash reports.
  • Permissions added by Google's libraries: the advertising ID and Android ad-services permissions (for ads and ad measurement), the Play install referrer (used by Firebase Analytics to know where an install came from), and wake-lock and background-work permissions used by Google's libraries to send their data reliably.

File Resurge does not ask for your contacts, calendar, location, camera, microphone, phone, SMS or accounts, and it does not show notifications.

Ads (Google AdMob)

File Resurge is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: banners on some screens, one native ad on the Home screen marked "Ad", a full-screen ad at most once every 90 seconds after a recovery or a clean-up has finished, and an app-open ad when you start or return to the app (never on the very first launch). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID, a resettable identifier used for advertising.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, Android version, language and app version.
  • Your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location.
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps, and diagnostic information about the ads.

Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

Before any ad is requested, File Resurge uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent where it is required, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and to offer choices where other privacy laws provide them. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices and on Google's policies.

To change your choice, open File Resurge, go to Settings and tap Privacy Options. This option appears when it applies in your region.

You can also manage how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

App statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

File Resurge uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used (for example app opens, screens viewed, app version, device model, Android version, country and the install source), and Firebase Crashlytics to receive reports when the app crashes (the stack trace, device model, Android version and app version). Firebase uses an app-instance identifier and may use the advertising ID where available. This data does not include your files, file names or file contents. It is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

What we don't do

  • We do not upload, view or keep your photos, videos, audio, documents or their names.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use your files for advertising.
  • File Resurge does not run in the background, show notifications or display ads outside the app.

Security

Data sent by Google's libraries (ads, consent, analytics and crash reports) is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Data retention and deletion

  • Scan results are removed when you close the app. Uninstalling File Resurge deletes its settings from your phone.
  • Recovered copies are ordinary files you own. You can delete them in File Resurge (Restore Data) or with any file manager; uninstalling the app does not remove them.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, and change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy Options.
  • Analytics and crash data held by Google are kept for the retention period set in Firebase and are then deleted automatically. Because this data is not linked to your name or account, we cannot look up an individual person in it. To ask for deletion of any data associated with your use of the app, use the developer contact option on the app's Google Play page.

Children

File Resurge is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, use the developer contact option on the app's Google Play page and we will help remove it where we can.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and its effective date before the new version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer contact option on the File Resurge Google Play page.
