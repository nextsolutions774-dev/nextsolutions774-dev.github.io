File Resurge

Find photos, videos, audio and documents that are still on your phone in hidden folders or the system trash, and save copies of the ones you want.

View File Resurge on Google Play

What File Resurge does

Two kinds of scan

"Scan all" lists every photo, video, audio file or document on your phone. "Scan deleted" lists only hidden copies (for example thumbnail caches) and items in the system trash.

Real results

Every file shown really exists on your phone. The scan shows a live count of the files found and can be cancelled at any time.

Recover copies

Select files and File Resurge saves copies in Pictures, Movies, Music or Documents, in a FileResurge folder your gallery and file manager can see. The originals are not changed.

Preview first

View photos, play videos and audio, or open documents in another app before you decide.

Clean Storage

Finds temporary and log files, thumbnail caches, files already in the trash, installer (APK) files and byte-for-byte duplicate photos. You see each file and its size, and nothing is deleted until you confirm.

Restore Data

Lists the copies you recovered, by type, so you can open, share or remove them.

What it cannot do

File Resurge can only find files that still exist on your phone, such as hidden copies and items in the trash. Files that were permanently erased or overwritten cannot be brought back by File Resurge.

File Resurge works in English, Deutsch, Español, Français, हिन्दी, Magyar, Português, Türkçe and Tiếng Việt, supports light and dark mode, and works on phones and tablets.

Permissions in plain words

All files access (Android 11 and later)

Hidden folders and trashed files can only be read with this access. File Resurge explains why before it opens the Android setting, and you can turn it off at any time. Scanning and recovery happen on your phone; your files are never uploaded.

Storage (Android 9 and 10)

On older Android versions the app asks for the standard storage permission for the same purpose.

Internet and network state

Used for ads and for app statistics and crash reports.

File Resurge does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, phone or accounts, and it does not send notifications. The privacy policy has the details.

Free, with ads

File Resurge is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first.
