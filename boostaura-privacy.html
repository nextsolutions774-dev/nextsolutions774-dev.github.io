BoostAura Privacy Policy

BoostAura 1.1.0 · Effective date: 4 November 2026

Summary

BoostAura is a phone utility: it shows your storage, helps you review and remove files you choose, shows battery information, measures your connection speed and plays a speaker-cleaning tone.

Everything BoostAura reads about your files, storage and battery is processed on your phone and is never uploaded. BoostAura has no account and no servers of its own. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to provide them, as described below.

Who we are

BoostAura (package name com.boostaura.phone.clean) is an app published on Google Play (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

What stays on your device

These things are read or stored only on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Storage totals (used and free space).
  • Battery level, status, temperature, voltage, health, charge counter and cycle count, as reported by Android.
  • Your connection type (Wi-Fi, mobile data, Ethernet or VPN). BoostAura does not read Wi-Fi network names and does not use location.
  • Your settings: theme, wallpaper colours, the large-file size for Clean Up, and when an ad was last shown (used only to limit how often ads appear).

Files and folders

Clean Up only works with folders you pick yourself in Android’s system folder picker. BoostAura cannot see any other files.

In those folders it reads file names, sizes, dates and types, and reads file contents only to compare possible duplicates; that comparison (a checksum) is calculated on your phone. File names, contents and checksums are never uploaded.

Nothing is deleted until you select it and confirm. For photos, videos and audio, Android asks you to confirm again. You can remove BoostAura’s access to a folder at any time in Clean Up, or by uninstalling the app.

Network Test

When you start a Network Test, BoostAura downloads and uploads test data (random bytes, up to about 35 MB in total) to Cloudflare’s public speed-test service at speed.cloudflare.com, and measures how long it takes.

No personal information is included. As with any internet connection, Cloudflare receives your IP address and basic connection details to deliver the data; Cloudflare’s own privacy policy (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy) applies to that. Results stay on your phone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

BoostAura is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen and, at most once every few minutes, a full-screen ad after a finished clean or network test.

To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising ID.
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version).
  • Your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location.
  • Ad interactions (views and taps) and diagnostic information about the ads.

Data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choices and Google’s policies. Google’s use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your consent choices

If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland) or where privacy laws give you choices about ads (such as some US states), BoostAura shows Google’s consent message the first time you open the app. You can change your choice later in Settings → Manage ad consent, which appears whenever it applies to you.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Permissions

BoostAura asks for very few permissions, and none that need your approval:

  • Internet: for the Network Test and for ads.
  • Network state: to show your connection type and know when you are offline.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Wake lock: used by the Google ads library.

BoostAura does not ask for access to your photos, media, contacts, location, phone, SMS, calendar or accounts, and does not use accessibility services.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t upload your files, file names or any information about them.
  • We don’t run our own servers, user accounts or analytics.
  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t claim to free RAM, kill other apps, cool your phone or remove viruses, and we don’t show fake warnings.

Crash and stability reports you may have agreed to share with Google through Android are collected by Google Play, not by BoostAura.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything BoostAura stores is kept on your phone in the app’s private storage. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling BoostAura deletes it. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google’s policies.

Security

BoostAura connects to the internet only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Folder access is limited to the folders you choose and can be revoked at any time.

Children

BoostAura is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the developer contact details on BoostAura's Google Play Store page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to processing, or to opt out of targeted advertising. Because BoostAura keeps your information only on your phone, you can delete it yourself by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling. For data Google collects for ads, use the consent choices above or contact Google. For anything else, contact us using the developer contact details on BoostAura's Google Play Store page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and significant changes will be explained in the app’s release notes.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us using the developer contact details shown on BoostAura's Google Play Store page.
