FileWash Privacy Policy

FileWash 1.0.6 and later · Effective date: 6 November 2026

Summary

FileWash (package name com.geigo.filewash.laji) is an Android utility published by Maza Consulting LLC ("we", "us"). It shows storage, battery and app usage information, lets you delete files you choose, measures your connection speed and plays test tones.

The information FileWash reads about your phone is processed on your phone and is not uploaded. FileWash has no accounts and no servers of its own. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to provide them, as described below.

Information processed on your device

FileWash reads the following only on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Storage statistics: total, used and free space, and each app's cache size if you turn on Usage Access.
  • App usage statistics: screen time and launch counts for your apps, if you turn on Usage Access, and the names of the apps you can launch.
  • Battery information: level, status, health, temperature, voltage, current and estimated capacity, as reported by Android.
  • Files you pick: the name and size of each file you select in Android's file picker. FileWash never uploads your files or their names, and it cannot see files you did not pick.
  • Connection status: whether you are online.

The only information FileWash stores long term is a small set of app settings, such as whether you have accepted this policy. It is kept in the app's private storage and is excluded from Android backups.

Deleting files

Nothing is deleted until you pick it and confirm. For photos, videos and audio on Android 11 and later, Android shows its own confirmation. FileWash then checks which files are actually gone and reports only that amount as freed. FileWash cannot delete the cache of other apps; it links you to each app's storage page in Android settings, where you can do that yourself.

Usage Access

Usage Access is optional and you control it in Android settings. You can turn it off at any time. Without it, the app cache list and Recent Apps stay empty and the other tools still work.

Network Test

When you start a Network Test, FileWash downloads about 10 MB of test data from Cloudflare's speed test service at speed.cloudflare.com, then uploads about 5 MB of blank test data to the same service, and measures how long each takes. No personal information or file content is included.

As with any internet connection, Cloudflare receives your IP address and basic connection details in order to deliver the data. The Cloudflare privacy policy applies to that. Your results are shown on your phone and are not sent to us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

FileWash is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the Home screen and, at most once every 5 minutes, a full-screen ad after a finished action. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID, a resettable identifier used for advertising.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, Android version, language and app version.
  • Your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location.
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps, and diagnostic information about the ads.

Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

FileWash uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent where it is required, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and to offer choices where privacy laws provide them. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices and on Google's policies.

To change your choice, open FileWash and tap Privacy choices on the Home screen. This option appears when it applies in your region.

You can also manage how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

Permissions

  • Internet: for ads and the Network Test.
  • Network state: to check whether you are online.
  • Usage Access (optional): for app cache sizes and Recent Apps.
  • Vibration: for the optional vibration in Speaker Tone.
  • Advertising ID and related permissions added by the Google ads library: for ads, as described above.

FileWash does not ask for media or all-files access, or for your contacts, calendar, location, phone or accounts.

What we don't do

  • FileWash has no user accounts.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't run our own servers or analytics for FileWash, and we don't receive the information listed under "Information processed on your device".

Children

FileWash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at the address below.

Keeping and deleting data

Uninstalling FileWash, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes everything the app stores on your phone. We hold no copy, because FileWash does not send this information to us. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies; you can manage it through the Google links above.

If you update from an older version of FileWash, the update deletes that version's leftover launcher data and settings from the app's storage once, the first time it runs.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to its processing, or to opt out of targeted advertising. For information on your phone, you can use the steps above. For ad data, use the choices above or contact Google. For anything else, contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top of this page shows when the current version took effect.
