FileWash

See your phone's real storage, battery and app usage, and delete the files you choose.

View FileWash on Google Play

What FileWash does

Home

Shows how much of your storage is used and how much is free, as reported by your phone.

Clean

Lists real cache sizes for each app (with Usage Access) and links to each app's storage page. Pick files to delete with Android's confirmation, open the system "Free up space" manager, and see the space actually freed.

Battery

Shows level, health, temperature, voltage and charging information, with live charts.

Recent Apps

Shows screen time and launch counts for your apps over the last hour, today, yesterday or the last 7 days (with Usage Access).

Network Test

Measures your connection's download and upload speed when you start a test.

Speaker Tone

Plays tones from 20 to 5000 Hz, with optional vibration.

Permissions in plain words

Usage Access (optional)

You turn this on yourself in Android settings. It lets FileWash read app cache sizes for Clean, and screen time and launch counts for Recent Apps. This information stays on your phone. Without it, those lists stay empty and the other tools still work.

Internet

Used to show ads and to run the Network Test.

No media or all-files permission

FileWash does not ask for access to your photos, media or all your files. It only works with files you pick yourself in Android's file picker.

FileWash also uses the network state permission to check your connection, vibration for the optional Speaker Tone setting, and the advertising ID for ads. The privacy policy has the details.
